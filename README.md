## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze bank customer churn. It helps identify customer segments with the highest churn risk by analyzing age groups, countries, product holdings, and member activity. The dashboard enables data-driven decisions to improve customer retention.

## Objectives
Analyze customer churn trends.
Identify high-risk customer segments.
Compare churn across countries and age groups.
Measure customer activity and account balance.
Provide actionable business recommendations.

## Dashboard Features
KPI Cards (Total Customers, Churn Rate, Average Balance, Active Members)
Customer Churn by Age Group
Customer Churn by Country
Customer Churn by Number of Products
Churn by Member Status (Donut Chart)
Interactive Slicers (Country, Age Group, Gender)
Custom Theme & Tooltip Support

## Key Insights
Customers aged 41–50 show the highest churn.
Germany records the highest customer churn.
Customers with only one product are more likely to leave.
Inactive members contribute significantly to overall churn.

## Business Recommendations
Focus retention campaigns on high-risk age groups.
Re-engage inactive customers with personalized offers.
Encourage customers to adopt multiple banking products.
Strengthen customer engagement programs in high-churn regions.
